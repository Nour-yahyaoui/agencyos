# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AgencyOS to be a welcoming, professional community. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces is expected to treat others with respect, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, inclusive and constructive, especially when giving or receiving feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not individuals. Assume good faith and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks and disruptive behavior.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Nour-yahyaoui). Do not post sensitive details publicly. The maintainer will review reports and may take proportionate action, such as requesting a change in behavior, removing content, or limiting participation. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical; no specific response time or confidentiality guarantee is implied.

Security vulnerabilities should instead be reported under the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
